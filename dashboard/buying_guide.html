Cricket Bat

Kashmir Willow / English Willow
Buy Now ▼

What to Check

  • Kashmir Willow for casual play, English Willow for serious cricket
  • Weight: 1100–1200g is ideal for most players
  • Grain count: 6–8 grains = good quality
  • Check for no cracks on the blade surface
  • Knocking required before first use
  • Handle grip should feel firm, not slippery

Types

  • Kashmir Willow — Budget, durable, casual play
  • English Willow Grade 3 — Mid-range, serious play
  • English Willow Grade 1 — Pro-level, premium price
  • Tennis ball bat — Lightweight, for gully cricket

Budget Range

₹400 (basic) Sweet spot: ₹800–1,500 ₹5,000+ (pro)

✅ Why Buy

Fitness, fun, stress relief. A good ₹1,000 bat lasts 2–3 years easily.

❌ Why Skip

If you never actually play and it just collects dust.

Where to Buy (Near Guduvanchery)

🏪 Sports shops on GST Road, Tambaram 🏪 Decathlon, Pallavaram / OMR 🌐 Amazon / Flipkart 🌐 SS Cricket official site

Custom Pull-up Bar

Wall-mounted / Doorframe / Freestanding
Buy Now ▼

What to Check

  • Weight capacity: min 120kg for safety margin
  • Wall-mounted = most stable, needs drilling
  • Doorframe = no drilling but less stable
  • Pipe thickness: 32mm–38mm is comfortable to grip
  • For custom: use GI pipe (galvanized iron), not PVC
  • Check wall material before mounting (RCC preferred)

Types

  • Doorframe bar — Easy install, budget option
  • Wall-mounted — Permanent, most stable
  • Freestanding tower — Expensive, no drilling needed
  • Custom GI pipe — Cheapest, made locally by welder

Budget Range

₹500 (custom) Sweet spot: ₹1,000–2,500 ₹8,000+ (freestanding)

✅ Why Buy

Best ROI fitness item. Pull-ups work back, arms, core. One-time cost, lifetime use. Custom from local welder = ₹500–800 max.

❌ Why Skip

Don't buy if renting and can't drill walls. Go doorframe type instead.

Where to Buy / Make

🔧 Local welder near Guduvanchery bus stand 🏪 Hardware shops, Tambaram 🌐 Amazon (doorframe type) 🌐 Decathlon.in

Carrom Board

Full size / Mini
Buy Now ▼

What to Check

  • Board size: Full (29×29 inch) for proper play
  • Plywood thickness: min 12mm, 18mm is best
  • Surface should be smooth — run hand to check
  • Legs should be sturdy, not wobbly
  • Check striker and coins quality
  • Boric powder needed for smooth play (buy separately)

Types

  • Tournament grade — 18mm ply, competition use
  • Standard full size — 12mm, home use
  • Mini board — Small, travel use

Budget Range

₹800 (basic) Sweet spot: ₹1,500–2,500 ₹5,000+ (tournament)

✅ Why Buy

Family entertainment, lasts decades. ₹1,500 buys a solid board that pays for itself in fun.

❌ Why Skip

If you live alone with no one to play with regularly.

Where to Buy

🏪 Toys/sports shops, Tambaram market 🏪 General stores, Guduvanchery 🌐 Amazon / Flipkart

Shoe Stand / Rack

3-tier / 4-tier metal or wood
Think Before ▼

What to Check

  • Number of tiers: 3-tier holds 6 pairs, 4-tier holds 8 pairs
  • Metal frame = more durable than plastic
  • Width: 60cm is standard, check your entry space
  • Stackable racks are better for expanding later
  • Avoid cheap plastic — breaks in 6 months

Types

  • Open metal rack — Budget, air circulation
  • Wooden cabinet — Premium look, closed storage
  • Stackable plastic — Cheap but breaks easily
  • Wall-mounted — Space saving, needs drilling

Budget Range

₹300 (plastic) Sweet spot: ₹500–900 ₹3,000+ (wood cabinet)

✅ Why Buy

Keeps home organized. ₹600 metal rack is all you need. Not exciting but useful.

❌ Why Skip

Not urgent. Buy last. Any local store has these — no need to overthink.

Where to Buy

🏪 Any household store, Guduvanchery / Tambaram 🌐 Amazon / Flipkart (check local pickup)

Cooling Glasses (Sunglasses)

UV protection / Polarized
Think Before ▼

What to Check

  • UV400 protection is mandatory — not just "UV protection"
  • Polarized lenses = reduces glare (good for Chennai sun + bike)
  • Frame fit: should not press nose bridge or ears
  • Lens color: grey/brown for outdoor, yellow for night
  • Check for CE or ANSI Z80.3 certification marking
  • Avoid cheap ₹100 roadside glasses — damage eyes more than protect

Types

  • Polarized — Best for outdoor/biking in Chennai sun
  • Photochromic — Darkens in sun, clears indoors
  • Sports wrap — For cricket/football
  • Fashion frame — Style over function

Budget Range

₹300 (fake UV) Sweet spot: ₹500–1,500 ₹5,000+ (Ray-Ban)

✅ Why Buy

Chennai heat is brutal. If you commute by bike, UV400 polarized glasses genuinely protect your eyes. ₹800 buys a solid pair.

❌ Why Skip

If you travel only by car/bus. Don't buy roadside ₹100 glasses — they do more harm than good.

Where to Buy

🏪 Lenskart store, Tambaram / Velachery 🏪 Optical shops on GST Road 🌐 Lenskart.com 🌐 Amazon (check seller carefully)

Wait / Skip ▼

  • Processor: Intel i5-12th gen or AMD Ryzen 5 5600 minimum
  • RAM: 16GB DDR4 minimum for multitasking
  • Storage: SSD (512GB) for speed, HDD for storage
  • Monitor: 24-inch FHD IPS panel minimum

  • Mid-range — i5/Ryzen 5, recommended for you
  • Workstation — i7/Ryzen 7, heavy work

Budget Range

₹20,000 (basic) Sweet spot: ₹35,000–50,000 ₹80,000+ (workstation)

✅ Why Buy

If you're serious about SSC prep, Raspberry Pi projects, MATLAB/Python practice at home — a PC makes all that possible.

❌ Why Skip

Most expensive item. At ₹20,000–35,000/month salary, this is 1–2 months income. Wait for salary hike or government job confirmation.

Raspberry Pi

Pi 4 / Pi 5 / Pi Zero
Buy Now ▼

What to Check

  • Pi 5 (latest) = best performance, 4GB/8GB RAM
  • Pi 4 = still very capable, cheaper now
  • Pi Zero 2W = budget, for lightweight IoT projects
  • Buy official kit with power adapter + SD card + case
  • MicroSD: min 32GB Class 10 for OS
  • Check for genuine boards — avoid clones on Amazon
  • Must buy from authorized Indian reseller

What You Can Build

  • Home automation (lights, fans via relay)
  • IoT energy meter (relevant to your project!)
  • SCADA dashboard on local network
  • Mini desktop PC / media server
  • Python + AI projects for resume
  • Dog detection system upgrade from your project

Budget Range

₹1,500 (Pi Zero) Sweet spot: Pi 4 kit ₹6,000–8,000 Pi 5 kit ₹10,000–13,000

✅ Why Buy

As an EEE engineer at Inovance, this is a DIRECT skill builder. IoT + automation projects on Raspberry Pi boost your resume massively. Much cheaper than a PC for learning.

❌ Why Skip

Don't buy if you won't invest time in learning. It's a project tool, not a toy.

Where to Buy

🏪 Robu.in / Evelta — physical pickup available 🏪 SP Road electronics shops, Chennai 🌐 Robu.in (most reliable in India) 🌐 Evelta.com (official Pi reseller) 🌐 Amazon (check seller — authorized only)

Drone

Hobby / Photography / Racing
Wait / Skip ▼

What to Check (if buying)

  • Under 250g = no DGCA registration needed in India
  • Above 250g = must register with DGCA (Digital Sky portal)
  • Flight time: 20–30 min per charge is decent
  • Range: 500m+ for photography drones
  • Camera: 2.7K minimum if buying for photography
  • Spare propellers and battery essential to buy with

Types

  • Toy drone (under ₹2,000) — indoor only, no camera
  • Mini drone under 250g — no registration, basic cam
  • DJI Mini 4 Pro — Best under 250g, expensive
  • FPV racing drone — DIY build, technical
  • Agriculture drone — Enterprise level, very expensive

Budget Range

₹1,500 (toy) Good drone: ₹15,000–30,000 DJI: ₹60,000+

✅ Why Buy

If you want to learn aerial robotics or build an FPV drone from scratch — great EEE project. Drone tech is a growing career field in India.

❌ Why Skip (Now)

⚠️ Legal issue: Flying drones in Guduvanchery/Chennai suburbs requires DGCA permission. Tamil Nadu has strict no-fly zones near airports (Chennai airport nearby!). Cheap toy drones are useless outdoors. Good drones cost ₹15,000–60,000. SKIP until financially stable.

Where to Buy (when ready)

🌐 DJI India official site 🌐 Amazon (check seller) 🏪 Drone shops, Anna Nagar, Chennai
